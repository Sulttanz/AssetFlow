---
name: Reports hub policy
description: Keep the Analytics & Reports hub as a navigation catalog rather than an embedded analytics screen.
---

The Reports hub is strictly an expandable index of four domains: Employee & Personal, IT Asset Management, Project Management & Governance, and ITSM Service Desk. Expanding a domain reveals report links only; metrics and detail tables belong on dedicated report pages.

**Why:** The user clarified that embedded employee metrics and tables made the main hub confusing, and requested the same category-and-report hierarchy in the sidebar.

**How to apply:** Keep report data queries out of the hub, preserve module and role gating for domain links, and retain dedicated destinations for every child card. Mark analytics without a completed data engine as Planned rather than showing invented metrics.
