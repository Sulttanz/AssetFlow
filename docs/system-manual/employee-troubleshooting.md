# Employee Troubleshooting & Ticket Submission

Use this guide when you cannot sign in, cannot find a project, or are unsure whether to report an incident or request a service.

## Sign-in problems

1. Confirm the username or email address is spelled correctly. Email sign-in works only when an email is recorded on your account.
2. Try the username if your account has no email address.
3. Confirm the account has not been disabled. Ask your administrator to check the account; do not create another login to bypass the restriction.
4. Never send your password in a ticket or Knowledge Base comment.

## Cannot see a project or project ticket

Project work is limited to the projects assigned to your user account. Ask an authorized user administrator or project manager to verify the **Assigned Projects** membership. Linking your account to an employee record does not automatically add project membership. If you are an external project user, internal My Assigned Assets is intentionally hidden.

## Choose the right intake

- Report a service interruption, defect, or broken equipment through **Report an incident** and choose the closest Incident Catalog type.
- Ask for new hardware, software, access, or a standard service through **Service Catalog**.
- Submit a **Project Change Request** only when the portal offers that action for a project assigned to you. It requires review and approval before an implementation task is created.

End users do not set priority, urgency, impact, assignment group, engineer, or SLA. Those values are applied by the service desk from the incident template and server policy.

## Follow up on a ticket

Open the ticket from **My Tickets** to check its current status. Incident details support comments and file attachments. Add a concise description, the affected service or device, when the issue began, and safe troubleshooting already attempted. Do not attach secrets, passwords, or unrelated employee records. For a service request, check its approval and fulfillment status in the request listing and contact the service desk if the displayed status needs clarification.

## Still blocked?

Include the page name, a short description of what you expected, the time of the issue, and any visible request/reference number when contacting your administrator or service desk. Do not include a password or authentication token.
